About

Building things nobody said was possible.

20+ years building things on the web — from small business sites to complex multi-tenant SaaS platforms that probably shouldn't work but stubbornly do. Led development teams, mentored junior developers who went on to remarkable things, and pushed the Silverstripe framework somewhere its creators likely never intended.

Earlier in my career I worked as an electrician's mate — domestic, commercial, PBX and network cabling installs. Those skills never left. You'll still find me building servers, architecting networks and soldering things that probably don't need soldering.

On the side: audio DSP and BPM analysis — port of Bram de Jong's BPM code that ended up in commercial mixing software, released quietly as BigJimSlizards. I also challenged my father to an audio analysis programming competition once. I believe I won. He disagreed on licensing grounds.

Currently building BookN — a scale-ready multi-client booking platform. The stuff that's interesting to me is in the architecture: single API endpoint, multiple client databases, Silverstripe's ORM pushed hard. Informed by hard lessons from previous high-traffic production incidents and a genuine love of the problem.
